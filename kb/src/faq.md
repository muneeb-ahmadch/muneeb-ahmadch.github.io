# Questions clients ask

## faq-hire-why | Why hire you with no Upwork reviews?
Because you can check the work instead of reading reviews: 13 measured experiments in a public repository, three live demos you can run, and this chat. I also start with a fixed-price diagnostic, so the first step is small and you keep the report either way.

## faq-stack | Which stack do you work in?
Python, FastAPI, LangGraph, LangChain, the OpenAI and Claude APIs, MCP, vector databases, Langfuse and Docker. React and Node when the fix reaches the frontend.

## faq-models | Which models do you work with?
OpenAI and Anthropic models through their APIs, and open-source models. This chat itself runs on Llama 3.1 8B.

## faq-timezone | Can you work in my timezone?
I'm in Lahore (UTC+5) and I answer messages every working day. Calls happen on Upwork's video tool at a clock time that works in your timezone.

## faq-fixed | Do you work fixed price or hourly?
Both. Hourly is $30 with the tracker on. For a defined job I quote one fixed price, usually in milestones, and the Project Catalog diagnostics have fixed prices from $95.

## faq-access | Do you need access to our production system?
No. A staging copy, a repository, or an endpoint with a test key is enough. I never need production credentials.

## faq-first-step | What is the first step?
A fixed-price diagnostic: I reproduce the failure, find the cause and write it up with the evidence. You decide whether to continue from there.

## faq-how-long | How long does a diagnostic take?
The Starter diagnostic takes 2 days, the Standard 3 days, and the Advanced, with an evaluation set and the first fix re-measured, 7 days.

## faq-wrong-answers | Our chatbot gives customers wrong answers. Can you help?
Yes, that is the problem I specialise in. I count how many of your real customer questions come back wrong today, fix the biggest cause, and re-run the same questions so you get a before and after table.

## faq-hallucination | Can you stop hallucinations completely?
I can measure them and push them down, and show you the number. On my own pipeline a better prompt took hallucinations on trap questions from 15 of 15 to 0 of 15. I won't promise zero on your system before I've measured it.

## faq-injection | Can you test our assistant for prompt injection?
Yes. I build working attacks against a copy of your own content, measure how often the model obeys them, check whether your guardrail can be fooled too, and rank the fixes. I never attack a production system.

## faq-evals | Can you build an evaluation set for us?
Yes. I build a golden set from your real questions, a scoring script, and a gate that fails when a change makes answers worse, the way my public harness works.

## faq-rag-build | Can you build a RAG assistant from scratch?
Yes, when it's a small assistant on your own documents and done can be measured as right answers out of a set of your real questions. The tests exist from the first day.

## faq-agents | Do you build AI agents?
Yes, in code: LangGraph agents and MCP servers. I don't build on no-code agent platforms.

## faq-fine-tune | Should we fine-tune instead?
My experiment 08 is about exactly that choice: whether a problem is missing knowledge or wrong behaviour, and when a fine-tune pays back. For wrong answers in a support bot, the cause is usually retrieval or instructions, not the model's knowledge, so I measure first.

## faq-cost | Can you reduce our LLM costs?
I measure cost per query first, the way I did in experiment 07 ($0.002174 per query, 2.1 seconds), and then look for the cause of the spend. I won't quote a saving before I've measured your system.

## faq-nda | Will you sign an NDA?
Yes, through Upwork, before starting.

## faq-team | Do you work alone or with a team?
I work alone. The person you talk to on Upwork is the person who does the work.

## faq-contract-to-hire | Are you open to a longer role?
Yes. I'm open to contract-to-hire and available for more than 30 hours a week.

## faq-reach | How do I contact you?
Message me on Upwork, or book the $40, 30-minute consultation from my Upwork profile. There's no email here on purpose.
