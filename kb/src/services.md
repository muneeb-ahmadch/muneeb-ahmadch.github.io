I never need production credentials. A staging copy, a repository, or an endpoint with a test key is enough. I work inside your repository or a private copy, I don't reuse your data, and I'm happy to sign an NDA through Upwork before starting.

## svc-guarantee | Guarantees
I can't guarantee a system is safe afterwards, and I'd be wary of anyone who does. On my own pipeline the defence took obedience from 8 of 18 to 1 of 18, and one attack still worked. I show you what is still open rather than hide it.

## svc-no-repro | If I can't reproduce the failure
